I build infrastructure and developer tooling for Kubernetes-based GPU cloud environments. My work sits between platform
engineering, infrastructure operations, and product engineering: deployment systems, service foundations, cluster interfaces,
observability, and the control-plane pieces that make large systems easier to operate.

At Lambda, I helped deploy the company's first Kubernetes cluster and scale its internal infrastructure to more than 15
clusters across cloud and on-premises datacenters. I now focus on helping product and infrastructure teams use Kubernetes
effectively, deploy reliably, and spend less time carrying operational detail in their heads.

My academic background is in philosophy, mathematics, and computer science, with graduate work in philosophy of science,
formal logic, and computability theory.
